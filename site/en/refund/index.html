Refund & Cancellation Policy

Version 1.1 — effective 23 November 2026

We want you to buy with confidence: try the software free for 14 days first, and if you buy and it does not suit you, these are the cases where we refund you.

• Prices depend on the customer's country as shown on the site; any refund is made in Egyptian pounds, as charged.

1. Before activation

If you ask to cancel within 14 days of payment, before using the order number to activate the software and before any installation or training: full refund.

2. After activation

Within 14 days of activation: we refund the yearly subscription (and extra branches) minus the services actually delivered (installation, training, data set-up, visits) at their listed prices, and the licence is cancelled.

If a defect stops you using a main part you subscribed to and we cannot fix it within 7 working days of your report: full licence refund, even after the 14 days, during the support period.

After 14 days from activation and with no defect: the licence price is not refundable.

3. Add-ons, renewals and services

An extra branch bought later: the same rules from its activation date.

Yearly renewal: refundable if requested within 7 days of its start (the previous expiry date), not after.

Services already delivered (set-up and training visit EGP 1,500, custom requests up to 3 changes EGP 1,500) are not refundable once delivered; a paid service not yet delivered is refunded in full.

4. How to ask for a refund

Send us on WhatsApp 01228827915 or 01121460640 or e-mail trioerp.corp@gmail.com: your order number, the phone you paid with and the reason.

We reply within 2 working days, and the money goes back the same way you paid (card or wallet) within 14 days of approval — also depending on your bank or wallet.

5. After a refund

The licence is cancelled and the software returns to an unactivated copy. Your data stays on your device and you can export it (Excel or a backup).

6. Your statutory rights

This policy does not reduce your rights under Egyptian Consumer Protection Law No. 181 of 2018.
