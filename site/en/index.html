          Frequently asked

          Before you order

          What do I need on the computer?

          A Windows 10 or 11 computer, plus a thermal printer and barcode scanner if you like. The program works offline; the internet is only for phone reports, online backup and updates.

          Can I try before I pay?

          Yes, a full 14 days free. When you pay it activates on the same computer and you keep everything you entered.

          Is my data safe?

          Your data stays on your shop's computer, with an automatic backup every 12 hours (and to Google Drive if you like) and a daily integrity check.

          What if I change computers?

          Your data moves easily, and we issue an activation code for the new computer.
